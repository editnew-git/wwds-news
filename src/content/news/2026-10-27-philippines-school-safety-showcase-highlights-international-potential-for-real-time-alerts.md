**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**GENERAL SANTOS CITY, Philippines — October 27, 2026 —** [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) Inc. is preparing to highlight a Philippines-based technology showcase demonstrating how digital-signage infrastructure could support real-time override and silent-alert communications in schools.

The showcase brings together participants in the Philippines, international collaborators, and trainees who helped prepare the technology for demonstration. Central to the project is a customizable remote-control interface designed to adapt the system’s appearance and controls for participating organizations and local requirements.

The initiative demonstrates how coordinated communication, technology, and international collaboration could support school-safety programs across borders. The Philippines showcase is part of a broader communications effort involving participants and media audiences in Canada, the United States, Jamaica, India, and the Philippines.

Worldwide Digital Signage also plans to explore opportunities to make the technology available to Canadian schools. Donation terms, participating schools, implementation timelines, and regulatory requirements have not yet been announced.

Video from the Philippines showcase is expected to accompany the release, giving audiences a closer look at the technology and the people supporting the initiative. Further implementation details will be announced as they are confirmed.
