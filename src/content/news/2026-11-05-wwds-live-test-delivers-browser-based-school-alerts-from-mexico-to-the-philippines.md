**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**November 5, 2026** — [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) (WWDS) has completed a live international demonstration of a browser-based emergency-alert system designed to display clear, timely safety instructions across connected screens.

During the demonstration, alerts were activated from a laptop in Mexico and delivered to test sign 3502 in the Philippines. Participants displayed three school-response messages—Shelter, Hold and Lockdown—and tested alert queuing, individual cancellation and the system’s all-clear state.

The demonstration showed how authorized users could direct alerts to multiple signs through a browser-based interface. A new alert could be placed above an existing message, while cancelling the newer alert returned the display to the preceding alert until it was also cleared.

WWDS is developing the technology for potential use in schools, universities and other locations that need visible emergency instructions. The browser-based approach is intended to support compatible display devices without requiring users to operate specialized screen-management tools.

The test also identified areas requiring further refinement. The display took approximately two minutes to become fully available after startup while its operating system, network connection and browser initialized. WWDS plans to add clearer startup feedback so users can see that the device is connecting. The team is also reviewing background-colour controls and a subscription-validation message encountered during testing.

The alert images tested during the demonstration included school-response terminology associated with The “I Love U Guys” Foundation. The demonstration does not imply an endorsement or formal partnership.

WWDS will continue refining and testing the interface before confirming availability, technical requirements or deployment plans.
