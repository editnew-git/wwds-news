**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**LAS VEGAS, Nevada, September 4, 2026** — The Worldwide Digital Sign Association announced that Matt O’Brien of [Wholeshot Digital](https://www.wholeshotdigital.com/) has agreed to join the organization as Founder Director.

O’Brien brings extensive digital-signage experience to the role. Through Ontario-based Wholeshot Digital, he supports organizations with digital-signage consulting, content management and deployment services.

As Founder Director, O’Brien will contribute to the association’s work in professional education, industry collaboration and practical support. He will also support Canadian outreach for a browser-based classroom clock and emergency-communication tool.

The tool is available as freeware at [911clock.com](http://911clock.com). Its browser-based design is intended to simplify access and support deployment across common classroom technology environments. Technical assistance and related implementation services may be handled separately.

O’Brien’s appointment strengthens the association’s effort to connect experienced professionals while advancing accessible digital-signage applications for education and public safety.

## About the Worldwide Digital Sign Association

The Worldwide Digital Sign Association is being developed as an independent organization focused on education, professional relationships, industry collaboration and practical support across the global digital-signage community.

## About Wholeshot Digital

Wholeshot Digital is an Ontario-based provider of digital-signage consulting, content management and deployment services. Learn more at [wholeshotdigital.com](http://wholeshotdigital.com).

## About WWDS

Worldwide Digital Signage Inc. (WWDS) is a leading provider of digital signage solutions for retail, transportation, and public venues. Our displays deliver real-time, high-impact communications where they matter most.
