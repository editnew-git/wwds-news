**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**PAGADIAN CITY, Philippines — November 10, 2026** — [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) today recognized Lord Dalinas and the Custard Tech Innovations team for presenting its classroom silent-alert concept to technology professionals in the Philippines.

The school-safety concept is designed to help authorized staff discreetly request assistance during an emergency without creating additional panic or drawing attention to the person initiating the alert. The presentation highlighted rapid notification, clear escalation procedures and resilient campus communications.

By presenting the concept to technology stakeholders, Custard Tech Innovations introduced a practical approach to classroom emergency communication and expanded awareness of its potential use in schools.

Worldwide Digital Signage congratulates Lord Dalinas and the Custard Tech Innovations team for helping bring greater visibility to school-safety technology in the Philippines.
