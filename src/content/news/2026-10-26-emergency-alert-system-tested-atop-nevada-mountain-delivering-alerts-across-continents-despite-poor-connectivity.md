**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**NEVADA — October 26, 2026** — Worldwide Digital Signage Inc. ([WWDS](https://wwds.co)) has completed a live demonstration of an Alyssa’s Law–focused silent emergency-alert capability using Zip from a mountaintop in Nevada.

During the demonstration, participants activated emergency commands including **Evacuate, Hold, Lockdown, Secure,** and **Alert**. Remote participants reported that the resulting display changes appeared in Pakistan and the Philippines in less than 0.2 seconds.

The reported response time has not been independently validated. However, participants reported that the emergency commands continued reaching the remote displays while the mountain team experienced weak connectivity, intermittent audio, poor weather and difficulty enabling its camera.

The demonstration highlighted the potential for rapidly activated safety communications across geographically dispersed locations. WWDS believes this type of technology could help schools simplify emergency-response procedures and make critical safety instructions immediately visible.

Supporting photographs and a post-production video are expected to be released following the demonstration. The team is also planning additional testing as it continues exploring practical pathways for school and classroom deployment.
