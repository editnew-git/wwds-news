**PHILIPPINES — November 18, 2026** — [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) is collaborating with technology partners in the Philippines to explore classroom tools that combine silent emergency alerts, visible emergency notifications and gamified learning experiences.

The initiative is intended to help authorized school personnel deliver clear instructions across classroom screens and other connected displays while supporting new ways for students to practise core skills. The participating team is preparing educational games with score tracking, including activities focused on mathematics and Philippine cultural themes.

The silent-alert concept is designed to allow authorized staff to request assistance discreetly and display approved emergency instructions on connected screens. Any school deployment would need to align with local safety protocols, technical requirements and government or district approvals.

Project collaborators are engaging education and government stakeholders about possible demonstrations and support. Development, testing and presentation plans remain in progress. No national rollout, legislation, public funding allocation or deployment timetable has been confirmed.
