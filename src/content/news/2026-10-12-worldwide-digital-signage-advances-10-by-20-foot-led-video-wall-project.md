**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**ESQUIMALT, British Columbia — October 12, 2026** — [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) is advancing work on a 10-foot-by-20-foot LED video wall for the Esquimalt Hockey Society.

Current preparations include testing and reprogramming LED panels, matching calibration coefficients to support consistent colour and brightness, and inspecting panel shaders to reduce colour bleed between pixels. The team is also developing screen-specific countdown-clock content and examining coordinated multi-screen controls through its SAM digital signage platform.

The project reflects Worldwide Digital Signage’s practical approach to large-format LED installations, bringing together hardware preparation, content development, and ongoing technical support to create a consistent viewing experience.

Additional project details, deployment timing, and approved imagery will be announced as work progresses.
