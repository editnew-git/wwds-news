*[Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) has begun alpha testing an emergency notification clock designed for classroom environments.*

Worldwide Digital Signage has begun alpha testing an emergency notification clock developed for classroom environments, with the first test scheduled to begin this week. The milestone will allow the company to evaluate the technology in a real-world classroom setting.

The clock is intended to help schools display timely emergency information through a familiar classroom fixture. A supporting landing page is also planned to highlight school-safety and active-shooter preparedness resources associated with the “I Love U Guys” Foundation’s work.

During the alpha phase, Worldwide Digital Signage will assess the equipment, classroom implementation process, and overall user experience. Feedback from the initial tester will help guide continued development and preparations for broader classroom use.

The initiative builds on the company’s experience supporting communication technology in complex institutional environments. Worldwide Digital Signage is also developing supporting dashboards intended to clarify operational roles and make future system management more accessible.

The alpha test is an early development milestone. Additional details about availability and future testing opportunities will be announced as the project progresses.

## About Worldwide Digital Signage

Worldwide Digital Signage develops digital communication solutions that help organizations deliver timely, relevant information across connected displays. Its work includes emergency communication, classroom technology, and tools designed to improve how critical messages reach communities.
