**LAS VEGAS, Nevada — August 12, 2026** — [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) is advancing a proof-of-concept demonstration for a low-power display solution designed to support wayfinding, operational notices and mission-critical public messaging.

The current work centres on sign 3582, which is being configured to display static content through an existing content-management layout. The demonstration is intended to explore how approved information can be distributed remotely to a connected display while maintaining a content format suited to low-power operation.

Unlike video-oriented digital signage, the proposed application focuses on information that changes only when required. Potential uses include school communications, transit wayfinding, municipal notices and emergency instructions. This approach could support locations where continuous power, frequent maintenance or full-motion displays are impractical.

The modular concept is also being explored for straightforward servicing and component replacement. The team is developing visual materials to explain the proposed system, its operational model and potential deployment scenarios to prospective collaborators and customers.

Worldwide Digital Signage will evaluate the demonstration results before confirming technical specifications, availability, commercial terms or deployment timelines.
