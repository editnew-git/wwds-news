**BRITISH COLUMBIA, CANADA — November 13, 2026** — [Worldwide Digital Signage](https://wwds.co) Inc. (WWDS) is launching a public-awareness and stakeholder outreach initiative focused on silent emergency-alert capabilities for classrooms across British Columbia.

The initiative is intended to help schools deliver clear, visible instructions during emergencies, including lockdowns, earthquakes and other situations in which audible announcements may be delayed, difficult to hear or inappropriate. WWDS says its technology can override connected classroom displays in under one second, allowing emergency instructions to appear directly on screens already used by teachers and students.

WWDS is encouraging discussions with provincial officials, school districts, educators, families and public-safety organizations about establishing consistent standards for silent classroom alerts. The company also plans to support the I Love U Guys Foundation’s Standard Response Protocol and explore how its display technology could complement established Canadian warning infrastructure, including Alert Ready and earthquake early-warning systems.

The proposed classroom program would use an alert-enabled clock or display and is intended to be made broadly accessible through a donation-funded model. WWDS estimates that basic teacher orientation could be completed in approximately five minutes. Deployment details, funding arrangements and technical integrations remain subject to consultation and validation with the appropriate authorities.

The British Columbia initiative forms part of a broader international effort to improve public access to timely emergency information. WWDS is also exploring opportunities related to classroom safety in the Philippines and missing-child alert practices demonstrated in Jamaica.

WWDS plans to begin outreach to government, education and public-safety stakeholders following the release of an accompanying informational video. The company is seeking dialogue on policy, implementation standards, funding and potential pilot opportunities.
